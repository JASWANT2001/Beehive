How engagement works

From first conversation to closing report, this is what an institution can expect.

01

Enquiry

You share the batch size, year group and calendar. We respond with a written proposal within two working days.

02

Baseline

Learners are assessed on speaking and writing before session one, so batches are grouped by level, not by age.

03

Delivery

Trainers run sessions on your campus, inside your timetable, with attendance and progress logged each session.

04

Report

A closing report covers level movement against the baseline, with individual learner notes for your records.

Common questions about our programmes

How long is a typical programme?

Odyssey runs across a full academic year. In-house and individual courses commonly run 50 hours. Crash courses run three days to two weeks depending on the target.

What does a session actually look like?

Sessions are built on speaking time. Learners work through role-plays, impromptu speaking, structured presentations and written tasks, with correction given in the moment rather than at the end.

Do you provide materials?

Yes. Workbooks and session material are provided as part of the programme fee, and are written for the level of the batch rather than pulled from a generic textbook.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on batch size, duration and location. Institutional pricing is quoted per learner. Send an enquiry and we will provide a written quotation.

Do you travel outside Tamil Nadu?

We deliver across Tamil Nadu on campus, and anywhere in India or abroad over video. Travel outside the state is possible for larger institutional engagements.

Not sure which programme fits?

Tell us who the learners are and what they need to be able to do. We will recommend the right one.
