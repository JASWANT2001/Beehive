Before you write to us

How quickly will I hear back?

Enquiries are answered within one working day. If you have asked for a proposal, expect the written document within two working days of the first conversation.

Can we arrange a demonstration session?

Yes. For institutional enquiries we can run a demonstration session with one class or one batch before you commit to a full programme.

Do you work with institutions outside Tamil Nadu?

We deliver on campus across Tamil Nadu, and anywhere in India or overseas over video. Travel beyond the state is arranged for larger engagements.

I want to join a course as an individual. Where do I start?

Select your course under enquiry type above and send the form. We will share the next batch start date, timings and fee structure.
